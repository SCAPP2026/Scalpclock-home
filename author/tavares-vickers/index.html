About Tavares Vickers

Founder & Creator, ScalpClock

Tavares Vickers is the founder and creator of ScalpClock, an interactive options trading education and practice platform. He built ScalpClock end-to-end — the live scalp signals board, the Learn Hub curriculum, Chart Replay, and the Exit Assistant — as a single place to learn trading concepts and then actually practice applying them.

Online, Tavares publishes under the handle @IamTVic. On ScalpClock, he writes the site's educational articles and guides on options trading, technical analysis, and trading psychology.

Why I Created ScalpClock

Learn, then actually practice

Many aspiring traders consume information — videos, articles, threads — without ever developing the ability to recognize and evaluate a setup in real time. Reading about a breakout and being able to spot one, live, on your own chart, are two different skills.

ScalpClock was built to close that gap by combining education with repeated, structured practice:

Learn→ Practice→ Analyze→ Review→ Improve

That loop — not any promise about results — is the idea behind every tool on the site.
