How to Use This

Run through this before entering a setup — during live trading, paper trading, or a Chart Replay session. Over time, if you notice you're consistently skipping the same item (say, never actually defining invalidation), that's useful information about a real gap in your process, not just a box left unchecked.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my checklist progress saved?
Yes, locally in your browser via localStorage — it's private to your device and this browser, and isn't sent anywhere or shared with ScalpClock.
Does checking every box guarantee a winning trade?
No. This is a pre-trade review habit meant to build a consistent process, not a signal or a promise of a specific outcome.
Should I use this checklist for every single trade?
Many traders find it most useful early on, while building the habit, and gradually internalize it — but returning to a literal checklist is also a reasonable way to reset discipline after a rough stretch.
Can I use this for stock trades too, not just options?
Yes — every item on this checklist describes the underlying stock's setup, so it applies whether you ultimately trade the stock or an option on it.
