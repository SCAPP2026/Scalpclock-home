    📖 How ORB Works
    OPENING RANGE → BREAKOUT → RETEST → CONFIRM → PLAN
    1. The Opening Range is established during the selected opening period (15/30/60 minutes at the start of the session).
    2. OR High = the highest price traded during the opening range.
    3. OR Low = the lowest price traded during the opening range.
    4. A bullish breakout requires a candle to close above OR High.
    5. A bearish breakout requires a candle to close below OR Low.
    6. A retest occurs when price returns to the broken OR level after the breakout.
    7. A confirmed bullish retest requires price to hold/reclaim OR High on a close.
    8. A confirmed bearish retest requires price to hold/reject OR Low on a close.
    9. Only after that confirmation does this engine classify the setup as a valid breakout/retest signal — a wick alone through either level is never enough.

    ORB Signal Engine FAQ

    Is the ORB Signal Engine free?
    Yes — it's free for everyone, no login or Pro plan required, including live 15-minute bars.
    What is an Opening Range Breakout (ORB) strategy?
    ORB is a strategy that trades a breakout above or below the high/low set in the first 15–60 minutes of the session (the "opening range"), typically waiting for a retest of that level before entering — the exact logic this tool runs live.
    Does a bullish or bearish signal here guarantee a winning trade?
    No. The Orb Score reflects conditions favoring buyers or sellers from six weighted indicators plus a confirmed breakout and retest — not a guarantee. Track your own win/loss tally and the Signal History outcome tracking for the tool's real, honest accuracy.
    What does the Orb Score actually measure?
    A weighted composite of six factors — Trend, Momentum, Volume, VWAP, Support/Resistance, and Pattern — recalculated every candle. It shows how aligned conditions currently are, not a prediction of what happens next.
