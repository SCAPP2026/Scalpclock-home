No-Trade Filter Checklist

  • Skip if the OR range is under ~0.15% of price — too tight, high whipsaw odds.
  • Skip if the OR range is over ~1.2% of price — reward-to-risk degrades fast.
  • Only take the first clean breakout each session per direction.
  • Require a strong-bodied breakout candle — fade dojis and thin wicks through the level.
  • No new entries once price has run 3+ candles past breakout with no retest.
  • Flatten any open position by the final candle of the session.
