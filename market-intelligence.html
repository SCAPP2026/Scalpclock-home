Why that reading? — SPY

CalculatedInterpretation

Eight checks, each scored +1 (bullish), 0 (no lean) or −1 (bearish). Three or more net points either way sets the bias. Confidence is Low, Medium or High depending on how much of the evidence agrees — it is not a win rate.

What is moving each ETF?

FactCalculated

An ETF moves because the stocks inside it move. A stock's pull on the ETF is its weight in the fund multiplied by how far it moved — so a giant company that is flat matters less than a mid-sized one that jumps.
