FAQ

Billing Questions

What's included in the Free plan?
Free includes the Learn Hub with daily lessons (2/day), market news, the economic calendar, a 5-stock watchlist, manual trade journaling, XP and leveling, and daily streak badges — no card required.
How does the Pro 5-day trial work?
Start a Pro trial with a card on file — you get full Pro access immediately and aren't charged until day 5. Cancel any time before then and you won't be billed at all.
When am I charged for Pro?
If you don't cancel before day 5, your card is charged the plan price you selected (monthly or annual) and your subscription continues automatically.
What happens if I cancel during my trial?
You won't be charged anything. You keep Pro access through the remainder of the trial period, then your account reverts to Free.
What is a Founding Member?
Founding Member is a limited offer for the first 500 ScalpClock traders: full Pro access at $1.99/month, locked for life, plus a personal referral link and recurring referral commissions.
How does the $1.99 Founding Member price work?
You're charged $1.99 today. As long as your subscription stays active, that price never increases — even after the 500 spots are gone and new subscribers pay full Pro price.
Is there a free trial for Founding Members?
No. Founding Member checkout charges $1.99 immediately, with full Pro access starting right after successful payment.
How does the referral program work?
Every Founding Member gets a personal referral link from their dashboard. When someone subscribes through it, the Founding Member earns a recurring monthly commission for as long as that referral stays subscribed, at the current rate shown in their referral dashboard.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes. There's no lock-in on any plan — cancel from your account settings whenever you want and you'll keep access through the end of the period you already paid for.
Can I switch plans?
Yes — you can switch between monthly and annual Pro billing from your account settings; the change takes effect on your next billing cycle.
Is ScalpClock financial advice?
No. ScalpClock is an education and practice platform built to help you learn options scalping concepts, practice setups, and analyze trades — it does not provide financial advice or guarantee trading outcomes.
